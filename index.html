概念

什么是 AI 原生基础设施？

面向 AI 工作负载，重新解释并扩展云原生系统的基础设施形态， 其算力、上下文与调度语义都发生了根本变化。

→ 了解从云原生到 AI 原生的演进
01
SUBSTRATE-COMPATIBLE
Built on Kubernetes and cloud-native systems, with AI-specific semantic extensions
02
NON-DETERMINISM
AI workloads are non-deterministic by nature
03
AGENT-FIRST
Agents, not services, are primary execution unit
04
FIRST-CLASS RESOURCES
GPU, context, and tokens become first-class resources
05
VALIDATION-FIRST
AI systems require continuous validation loops for runtime correctness
06
GOVERNANCE > DEPLOY
Scheduling is the primary control plane; deployment is secondary
